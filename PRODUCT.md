# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Employers and recruiters** evaluating Jasmine Vir for an electrical engineering role. They scan for discipline, tools, and track record, usually on a laptop between other candidates.
- **Prospective clients** deciding whether to hire her for engineering work. They need to see what she offers, evidence she can deliver, and an easy way to reach her.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio site of Jasmine Vir, electrical engineer. It introduces her, shows her experience and the services she offers, and turns interested visitors into a conversation (contact). Success: a visitor understands her specialty within seconds and reaches out.

## Positioning

An engineer's portfolio that reads like her own work product: the site presents itself as a drawing set rather than a generic résumé page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single scrolling page: loading screen, Home, About, Experience, Services, Contact. Nav jumps between sections.
- Scroll-driven animation is a requirement.
- Disciplines to represent: power systems, general electrical design, AutoCAD drafting, electronics, embedded systems.
- Contact method (form vs mailto) is undecided; no backend exists yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual theme is pinned by the user: **blueprint**, because she is an engineer.
- Must feel creative and authored, not templated.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. All biography, experience entries, projects, services, credentials, and contact details are placeholders the user will fill in later. Do not invent employers, certifications, licenses (e.g. PE), clients, metrics, or testimonials; placeholders must read clearly as placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. The site is her drawing set: structure and detail demonstrate engineering rigor.
2. Placeholders are honest and easy to replace; content lives in one data file.
3. Serve both audiences on one path: who she is, what she has done, what she can do for you, how to reach her.
